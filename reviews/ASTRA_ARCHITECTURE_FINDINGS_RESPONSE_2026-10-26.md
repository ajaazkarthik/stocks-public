## Five architecture findings — review-derived facts

| Dimension | What Astra's visible review actually argues | Concrete implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Forecast reliability (P0)** | Eight equally weighted historical underlying pathways with one uniformly proportional, constant-through-scenario IV shift across all exact legs are *plausible scenarios*, **not calibrated future option implied-volatility probabilities**. A fixed **$20** error deduction and **$15** hurdle together require at least **$35 modeled net** to clear the screen but do **not** provide calibrated uncertainty bounds. Eight scenarios make a reported worst-10% tail just the worst single observation. Prior same-contract IV-change experiment nominal 80% interval covered roughly **67%**, not a positive forecast result. | Introduce shadow *forecast-vintage registry* and matched no-purchase benchmarks. Track target dates, model inputs, calibration and prediction errors on subsequent observations, including unchanged IV and simple spot forecast controls; never relabel RV as future IV. |
| **Ranking/allocation (P0)** | Current ranking orders **conservative modeled net profit divided by contractual max loss**, not dollars of expected account-level net profit. A $30 / $140-risk spread ranks above a $45 / $225-risk spread, despite both fitting one contract in a $250 individual-position limit. Neither ordering is intrinsically correct until portfolio objective/risk appetite is specified. Correlated SPY/QQQ/IWM exposures (historical ~0.86 daily stock-return correlation) complicate independent decisions. | Keep the per-contract ratio for screening if useful but separate it from **integer portfolio allocation** using cash, already-reserved risk, loss per spread, contract quantities, collateral, holding duration and correlated market/IV/spread-widening stress; require a cash-abstention choice. |
| **Fair comparison (P0)** | F-01 and F-02 engineering fixes prevent expired marks and dropped candidates, but still **do not** replay entry confirmation, allocation, earlier stops/targets, or complete exits. Both arms require same information at decision, capital, fills/exits/cost methodology, and **later independently observed** reference outcomes (not model-selected winning paths). Keep two comparisons: **selection-only** (matched exposure/participation/exit policy) then **full-policy** (including new limits, abstention and cash). | Build an independent forward **decision + entry/close/fill-state lifecycle evaluator**. If actual matched quotes/fills unavailable, mark unresolved—not invented net profit. Report separately hypothetical reference mark, simulated fill, and broker confirmed fill. |
| **Full-spread economics (P1)** | Valuing both legs, bid/ask, fees and path-based exit rules is a good basis, but **zero** interest, dividend, funding and assignment assumptions are not automatically conservative for early-exercise ETF options. Daily closes cannot establish which intraday target/stop fired first; doubling current spread width does not validate stressed liquidity. Economically equivalent debit/credit expressions still need exercise-aware and consistent dividend-adjusted spot modeling. | Expose a standardized *assumption vector* and stress alternative rates/dividends/early-assignment/borrow/intraday path order and bid/ask widening. Compare payoff-equivalent positions fairly, do not imply fills or favor debit/credit merely by name. |
| **Price limits and source evidence (P1)** | Recomputing candidate exit mechanics at every suggested entry price is correct. Price limit is **conditional**, not executable fair value; retain disjoint/nonmonotone feasible regions. Refresh scenario input and exact quote/size/clock before any eventual trade. More provider feeds do **not** turn delayed side data into synchronized executable quotes. | Retain model-only limit preview and source-provenance classification. Keep forecasts, reference quotes, hypothetical fills and broker fills in four distinct data types and never silently upgrade them through joining. |

## Decision: retain engines, redesign three *research* layers next

**Not a profitability endorsement.** This is a proposed response to Astra, not a completed implementation or independently tested return. Existing code-level F-01/F-02 fixes stay closed; do not ask Astra to review line-by-line code. Astra should assess whether these architectural conditions would meaningfully falsify the thesis.

### Architecture milestone A — forecast registry and falsifiable calibration (P0)

- Save immutable **as-of timestamp, exact contract key/expiry, underlying/IV reference values, path weights/selection and model version** for every available no-purchase forecast. Distinguish original observation clocks from derived forecast creation/forward target clocks. Append later realized *observable* exact-contract IV/price outcomes without overwriting earlier predictions; no future features.
- Build **same-vintage comparator arms**: (i) zero spot drift with unchanged exact-leg IV, (ii) simple 5/20-day underlying drift/momentum with unchanged IV, (iii) optionally a simple conditional realized-vol regime assumption, and (iv) existing eight-path reference. Keep identical expiry/time horizon/fee/carry/entry assumptions across arms.
- Evaluate predictive error and interval coverage on **independent later sessions**, grouped by date and instrument rather than counting repeated strikes as independent experiments. For forecast net-profit comparisons, absence of future exact contract quotes is **MISSING_OUTCOME**, not a fake fill. Existing 1,145 IV pairs are a negative, sparse short-horizon diagnostic (738 trained/407 holdout across only three dates in holdout).
- **Acceptance:** forecasts and outcomes can be replayed with no as-of leakage; comparisons report denominator, observed/missing sessions, naive baseline, absolute errors, empirical interval coverage and model uncertainty. Do **not** claim calibration or real expected net profit without evidence.

### Architecture milestone B — joint integer portfolio allocator (P0)

- Inputs: each candidate's conservative *per-contract USD* expected net, max contractual loss, quantity bound, collateral/fee/cash reserve, underlying/direction/expiry and scenario tail exposures; existing reserved positions and exact timestamp. Reject contracts with unknown risk/price clocks rather than treat missing as zero.
- Feasible integer allocations (`q_i>=0`) must obey **per-trade contractual max-loss <= $250** and **combined reserved risk <= $1,000**, plus cash/collateral available and explicit cross-SPY/QQQ/IWM market/IV/liq stress limits. Zero positions (cash) always feasible. Where broker collateral/leg assignment uncertainty is unmeasured, label the constraint **NOT_EVALUATED**, not zero.
- Test counterexample: one $30 modeled-net/$140 loss spread versus one $45 modeled-net/$225 loss spread; ratio chooses the former but risk-budgeted account-level USD objective can choose the latter. Define a robust objective (total conservative modeled net minus explicitly budgeted tail and concentration penalties) and validate no allocation breaches.
- **Acceptance:** reproducible integer allocations, explicit no-trade cases, deterministic equal-score ties, transparent budget utilization, reason for declined instruments, and no live orders.

### Architecture milestone C — paired decision-to-outcome evaluator (P0)

- **Arm 1 — ranking only:** same frozen candidate set, decision/as-of information, entry participation, quantities, capital reserves and frozen exits; change *only* which eligible spread is ranked highest. Include eligible random/naive and cash controls at same decision times.
- **Arm 2 — full research policy:** allow model price limits, abstention, quantity allocation, shared risk budget and new policy; compare against original funded frozen scanner with its actual unchanged rules. Do **NOT** attribute changes in trade frequency to superior forecasting.
- Maintain independent state transitions: **alerted / reference quoted / hypothetically entry-priced / filled (only broker ACK) / expired / unresolved / closed (only documented evidence)**. A stale/no-side-price observation means unresolved; earlier target/stop without intraday data cannot be inferred using next-day price.
- **Acceptance:** position lifecycle comparison reports cash exposure, participation rate, time in market, realized broker-only net P/L when present, unresolved counts, and fully labeled *hypothetical* net return separately; source/fee/clock parity checks block invalid pairings. Required ≥20 new qualified sessions and ≥50 genuinely closed positions/arm remain preliminary descriptive gates for performance, never an engineering-development embargo.

### P1 controls — refine economics without buying data

- Explicit rate/dividend/exercise funding/stressed spread assumptions; report results across credible ranges instead of hardcoding zeros as conservative.
- Preserve monotonic/nonmonotonic price feasibility; stale-source and new quote revalidation. Stress earlier intraday path order as an **uncertainty band**, not an observed sequence.
- Evaluate whether selection is robust to crash paths, spread widening, implied-volatility term/smile change and correlated exposures, not only eight normal-ish equal-weight stories.

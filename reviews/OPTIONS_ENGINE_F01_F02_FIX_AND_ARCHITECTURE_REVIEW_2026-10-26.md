## What was fixed and how to falsify it

### F-01 — incorrect hypothetical quote marks after exit horizon/expiry

- Prior failure: `tradingview/options/shadow_repricing.py::one_arm` computed a hypothetical reference exit at **22 calendar days after decision**, or even **after actual option expiry**, although original `us-vertical-paper-v1` requires an exit by 21 calendar days / 7 days before expiration, and returns UNRESOLVED if price is missing.
- Corrective behavior: `one_arm` now checks the actual option expiry under the NYSE local date, rejects stale followups once expiration day begins, and rejects after the first 7-DTE exit opportunity has passed. `reconcile` now **passes each original saved `observed_at` as `decision_at`** into BOTH the unchanged-baseline and challenger arm; the reference return is **not calculated** when `now > decision_at + max_hold_days`. A quote before/at the horizon remains an **unverified hypothetical crossed bid/ask sensitivity**, never a historical broker fill or a reproduced complete lifecycle. Missing exact leg, wrong basis or unsynchronized leg update clocks remain fail-closed.
- Conservative timing: the saved **decision clock** rather than an unobserved hypothetical fill time is used for the 21-day ceiling. This can reject a late mark early; it intentionally does not assume an unobserved entry was actually filled.
- Research-only status examples: `EXPIRED_OR_PAST_HORIZON`, `PAST_MANDATORY_PRE_EXPIRY_EXIT_WINDOW`, `NO_POST_DECISION_QUOTE`, `LEG_QUOTE_TIMES_NOT_SYNCHRONIZED`. Invalid result has SQL reference net = **NULL** and observed fill always `NO_BROKER_FILL_EVIDENCE`. No option mark is imputed.
- Tests now require ordinary PASS (no XFAIL) for after-expiry, +22-day, past 7-DTE and actual journal reconciliation using original `observed_at`; it is impossible in these scenarios to write a nonnull hypothetical reference P/L.

**Scope limit for Astra:** This repair prevents misleading **out-of-window** hypothetical marks; it does **NOT** establish a true frozen-strategy state-machine replay, first entry fill, stop/target path, complete 15-minute reference mark series or actual matched filled exits. `shadow_reference_reprices` is still an observational reference **stress/sensitivity measure**, and must **NOT** be presented as realized baseline/challenger strategy performance.

### F-02 — 12-eligible-candidate hard cap discarded the entire challenger

- Prior failure: `existing_data_shadow.safe_comparison` passed `max_candidates=12` to `edge_engine.compare_frozen_to_edge`. If 13 or more frozen-eligible verticals existed, the entire challenger ranking returned `CANDIDATE_SET_EXCEEDS_RESEARCH_LIMIT` and no ranked opportunity, while the frozen chooser continued to work. Prior synthetic regression found **132** eligible.
- Corrective behavior: the existing `max_candidates` argument is now an explicitly **validated positive evaluation BATCH SIZE**, not a total cap. `compare_frozen_to_edge` accounts for the complete eligibility set, evaluates each candidate exactly once in sequential chunks, preserves each outcome and rejection reason, and sorts all evaluated results globally by conservative expected-net per unit of risk and exact leg identity. Full exact-contract candidate universe and frozen original `choose_candidate` stay unchanged. `evaluation_batch_size`, `evaluation_batches`, and `candidate_coverage=ALL_ELIGIBLE_CANDIDATES_ACCOUNTED_FOR` expose processing coverage; no unsupported `NO_EDGE` conclusion is generated because of an arbitrary 12 threshold. When forecast is absent, the correct status is `MISSING_POINT_IN_TIME_JOINT_FORECAST`, NOT an exhausted candidate-limit status.
- Crucially, the existing cent-grid price-limit search still processes every eligible spread that is valued and **continues to re-evaluate path-dependent stop/target exits at each hypothetical price**; there is no 'first 12 cheapest wins' shortcut. All selections remain experimental `notifier_eligible=False`, `paper_orders=0`.
- Tests now assert ordinary PASS on **13 / 50 / 132** eligible examples with stubbed synthetic valuations, exact once valuation per candidate, correct batch count, full result rank and no duplicate/missing spreads; the originally failing 132-candidate no-forecast test also passes.

**Scope limit for Astra:** Batching does not mathematically reduce the price-search complexity: evaluating `N` spreads across a ~`W*100` quote-cent grid and all `S` scenario paths with `T` projected timestamps remains roughly **O(N · W · S · T)** model evaluations. We have not proven low latency for an enormous live chain, and **no production alert engine was enabled**. Ask Astra to evaluate a principled architecture for pruning using *provable bounds* that do not bias the economic ranking, separate price-limit computation and meaningful out-of-sample objective tests. This is architecture, not a request to rewrite the code.

## What Astra should review (architecture / engine / economics)

1. **Selection objective & economic edge:** Is `expected modeled NET profit` (after ask/bid crossings, entry/exit slippage, commissions, funding/assignment assumptions, error allowance, and uncertainty) the right primary objective versus risk-adjusted expected net, exposure-neutral opportunity cost, and cash? Could reward/risk ratios conceal correlated tail losses?
2. **Two option-opportunity families:** Defined-risk credit spreads and directional debit spreads can express economically equivalent exposures at identical strikes. Is family classification based on **projected economic payoff** rather than the label "buy premium" vs "sell premium"? Are complete payoff and put/call parity assumptions coherent?
3. **Joint future underlying + option-implied volatility distribution:** Are the point-in-time model inputs, source vintage clocks, strike/expiration IV surface, projected dividends, early assignment/borrow/funding, and full stress paths economically sufficient? What minimal non-purchased free-source validation is credible? Historical underlying RV and current IV are **not the same** as independently calibrated future IV. The existing proxy is expressly `REFERENCE_PROXY_UNVERIFIED`.
4. **Portfolio/risk engine architecture:** How to allocate a $250 contractual-loss cap per spread and $1,000 combined reserve across SPY/QQQ/IWM when historical daily underlying co-movement is ~0.859, including correlated market crashes and intraday spread widening? How to enforce capital and simultaneous signal prioritization rather than summing isolated opportunity scores?
5. **Price-dependent alerts, exits and abstentions:** Is the max DEBIT / min CREDIT price limit at the current exact contract, decision deadline and all relevant execution rules economically consistent when entry price changes stop/target mechanics? Should the engine remain silent when edge is consumed and rank **cash** fairly?
6. **Causal evaluation design and confounding:** Keep original frozen universe, quote clock, capital, exits and outcomes identical between baseline and challenger. What measurable *forward* benchmark and exposure-matched random/naive competitors should be used, with no paid dataset, and which metrics would actually falsify the expected-net ranking thesis? Historical option fill P/L is **not measured**. No sample/Sharpe/alpha claim should be invented.
7. **Operational research-to-live boundaries:** Does the model-only / source-quality / quote-freshness / reviewer-evidence architecture clearly isolate experimental research from order routing, actionable Telegram and actual fills? Missing values must not be replaced by guessed historical IV/market quotes.

**Astra deliverable requested:** a concise **architecture and financial-engine critique** with ranked design problems, objective/forecast/risk-model alternatives, reproducible falsification experiments using the *existing* TV scan2/Yahoo/free Cboe/Moomoo/TQS/archive data, and an explicit decision: retain architecture, redesign components or reject edge thesis. **Do not spend its review on routine code-style issues, unit-test cosmetics or line-by-line implementation.** The engineering corrections F-01/F-02 are handled here.
